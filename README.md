Entry for the make.fun bounty
[One Word for the Boss](https://make.fun/bounties/u-swift-badger-44-one-word-for-the-boss-6b48c735):
exactly one word describing the boss.

## The entry

**Unskippable**

Like an unskippable ad: his monologues, check-ins and "quick calls" cannot
be skipped, muted or closed — and everyone has sat through them. The word
was chosen by duelling twelve candidates against the judge's published
criteria (see `candidates.md`); the runner-up was "Buffering".

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the entry word and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, where the
competition's program/v1 harness reads the artifact (gate: UTF-8 text of
1 to 30 characters, a single word).
